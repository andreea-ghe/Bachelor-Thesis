The Problem of Fracture Assembly

Given a set of broken 3D fragments, the goal is to recover the rigid transformation (rotation + translation) that reassembles each piece into the original object — a 6-DoF pose regression problem over point clouds.

Unlike semantic part assembly, where pieces have known functional roles, fracture assembly relies purely on local and global geometric cues: irregular break surfaces, concavity patterns, and surface continuity across fragments.

Why is it hard?

  • Combinatorial explosion — each piece can be in any orientation
  • Noisy, ambiguous fracture surfaces
  • Pairwise alignment errors drift when chained globally
  • Real-world fragments suffer erosion and missing parts

Applications span archaeology (fresco reassembly), medical imaging (skull reconstruction), and quality control in manufacturing.
