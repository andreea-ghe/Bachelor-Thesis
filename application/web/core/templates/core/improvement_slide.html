Proposed Modifications

Four targeted, modular extensions addressing complementary stages of the pipeline: feature interaction, feature refinement, neighborhood construction, and pose recovery.

{# Pair Attention #}

Pair Geometric Attention

Inspired by GPAT, we inject a learnable scalar bias into cross-attention logits, derived from inter-centroid RBF distances and triplet angles via a small MLP.

In Jigsaw's preprocessing context, the geometric descriptors have low variance, effectively acting as a learned soft regularizer that increases softmax temperature and produces more uniform attention distributions.

{# Double Attention #}

Gated Double Attention

Inspired by PHFormer and GARF's alternating attention strategy, we add a second self + cross-attention pass controlled by zero-initialized gates (ReZero): output = first-pass + g · residual.

At init, the model is identical to the pretrained baseline. The cross-attention gate learns a negative value, subtracting first-pass patterns rather than purely adding.

{# Gabriel Graph #}

Gabriel Graph Filtering

PointNet++'s kNN grouping is topology-unaware: neighbors may connect across interior voids. We apply Gabriel graph filtering to prune edges whose midpoint sphere contains other points, producing surface-faithful neighborhoods.

A minimum retention ratio prevents over-pruning in sparse regions.

{# ICP #}

ICP Refinement

RANSAC recovers poses from sparse correspondences, often leaving small residual misalignments. We insert dense point-to-point ICP between RANSAC and Shonan averaging to refine each pairwise transform.

Consistently reduces chamfer distance by +1 pp across all configurations.
