Key finding

Restoring ~37% of missing training data yielded a ~10-point PA gain — larger than any architectural modification. Double attention acted as an implicit regularizer on incomplete data, but gains vanished once full data was available. Loss scheduling sensitivity was a recurring factor across all experiments.
