Conclusions & Future Work

Conclusions

Data quantity is the dominant factor for reconstruction quality. But when data is scarce, architectural extensions act as implicit regularizers in multiple directions: architecture and data address the same representational bottleneck from complementary angles.

  • Gated double attention: +2.6 pp PA on cross-domain data, consistent across piece counts
  • Gabriel filtering: higher-quality matches, ~10% CD reduction on artifact, with lower computational cost
  • ICP: stable +1 pp chamfer improvement with no new parameters
  • Pair attention: learned temperature scaling that regularizes attention distributions

Backbone Upgrade

Replace PointNet++ with Point Transformer V3 (PTv3) for stronger geometric representations and native attention over point clouds.

Scale to 2 to 20 Pieces

The current evaluation covers 2 to 4 pieces. Testing on richer combinatorial diversity may reveal benefits invisible in the small-piece regime.

Efficient Attention

FlashAttention or PMTR's proxy-level matching could remove the quadratic bottleneck that limits scalability to larger piece counts.

Synthetic → Real Transfer

Breaking Bad lacks erosion and missing parts found in real archaeological data. Real-world evaluation on the FRACTURA dataset remains an open challenge.

Threats to Validity

  • All experiments use synthetically fractured meshes
  • Gabriel filtering was not jointly trained from scratch
  • Pair attention's regularization depends on Jigsaw's preprocessing
